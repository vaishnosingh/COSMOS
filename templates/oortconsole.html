Native Client Connection Instructions
Step 1 — Open Mornye Settings
Launch Mornye on your phone or desktop. Go to Sources > Add Sovereign NAS.
Step 2 — Paste WebDAV or Playlist URL
Enter the WebDAV URL or paste the Master M3U playlist URL generated above. Mornye will automatically index tags, album art, and lossless audio tracks.
Step 3 — Enjoy Lossless Sovereign Streaming
Your entire music library streams with instant scrubbing, caching, and background playback.
Step 1 — Connect to Server
In macOS Finder, press Cmd + K or click Go > Connect to Server.
Step 2 — Enter WebDAV Address
Paste your WebDAV URL: https://cosmos.linuxguy.space/oort/username/mybucket/
Step 3 — Authenticate
Select "Registered User". Enter your Cosmos username and Secret Key as the password. Your storage mounts as a native local drive!
Step 1 — Map Network Drive
Open File Explorer, right-click on This PC, and select Map network drive...
Step 2 — Enter Folder Address
Click "Connect to a Web site that you can use to store your documents and pictures". Enter your WebDAV URL.
Step 3 — Credentials
Provide your Cosmos username and Secret Key. The drive appears in "This PC" alongside your internal disks.
Mounting with davfs2 or rclone
sudo mount -t davfs https://cosmos.linuxguy.space/oort/username/mybucket/ /mnt/cosmos
# Or stream directly in VLC:
vlc https://cosmos.linuxguy.space/oort/username/mybucket/playlist.m3u
iOS & Android Audio Players
Compatible with Symfonium, Feishin, VLC, and Infuse. Simply choose WebDAV connection and paste your credentials.
